Before you submit, you can have an AI assistant read your manuscript and point out formatting problems, spelling and grammar slips, and places where the methods or results look incomplete — a missing sample size, an unnamed statistical test, an aim with no matching result.

What this is not. It is not peer review and it plays no part in the editorial decision. Nothing it says is shown to your editor or reviewers, and nothing it says is stored against your submission. Ignoring it entirely will not count against you.

The check runs inside Aaranya Scholarly's own Google Cloud project. Your file is sent over an encrypted connection, processed, and the result returned — it is not added to any training set, not reviewed by anyone at Google, and not stored by us beyond your browser session.

We deliberately use Vertex AI rather than the free consumer Gemini service, because the consumer terms permit Google to use submitted content for product improvement. That would be inappropriate for unpublished research.

If your co-authors, funder or institution have not agreed to your manuscript being processed this way, do not run the check. Your submission is unaffected either way.
