Scope of the Journal

ALSTM accelerates the translation of fundamental scientific discoveries into practical applications that improve human health, diagnostics, therapeutics, and healthcare systems — integrating molecular biology, biochemistry, biophysics, pharmacology, and computational science with clinical practice.

1

Biological & Biomedical Sciences

Molecular & cellular biology, genomics/epigenetics, systems biology, microbiology, immunology.

2

Chemical & Molecular Sciences

Biochemistry, medicinal chemistry, pharmacology & toxicology, analytical chemistry.

3

Physical Sciences in Life & Medicine

Biophysics, medical imaging, nanomedicine, quantum biology, biomedical materials.

4

Translational & Clinical Research

Precision healthcare, biomarker discovery, drug development, clinical trials (I–IV).

5

Computational & Digital Life Sciences

Bioinformatics, AI/ML in healthcare, systems medicine, digital health technologies.

6

Public Health & Emerging Areas

Epidemiology, regenerative medicine, One Health, environmental health.

Article Types Welcomed

Original Research Articles Review Articles Short Communications Case Reports Systematic Reviews & Meta-Analyses Editorials & Perspectives Letters to the Editor Technical Notes & Protocols Data Papers Methods Papers

Author Guidelines & Editorial Workflow

Every manuscript submitted to ALSTM follows a structured, transparent path from submission to publication. Prepare your manuscript per standard scholarly formatting (structured abstract, keywords, IMRaD body, references, declarations), then submit online for editorial processing.

1

Manuscript Submission

Author submits the manuscript online with cover letter and declarations.

2

Editorial Screening

Editor-in-Chief checks scope fit, formatting, and plagiarism screening.

3

Double-Blind Peer Review

Independent reviewers assess originality, rigor, and significance.

4

Editorial Decision

Accept, minor/major revision, or reject — communicated to the author.

5

Revision & Resubmission

Authors respond to reviewer comments and resubmit the revised manuscript.

6

Publication & Indexing

Accepted articles are copyedited, published online, and submitted for indexing.
