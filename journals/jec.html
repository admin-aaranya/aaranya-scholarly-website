Scope of the Journal

JEC fosters the integration and convergence of diverse engineering disciplines, bridging fundamental research with real-world applications, and encouraging work that combines multiple engineering domains toward sustainable systems and intelligent infrastructure.

1

Core Engineering Disciplines

Mechanical, civil/structural, electrical/electronics, chemical, materials, aerospace & automotive.

2

Computational & Digital Engineering

AI/ML, data science, IoT & cyber-physical systems, embedded systems & robotics, digital twins.

3

Emerging & Interdisciplinary Engineering

Mechatronics, nanotechnology, bioengineering, renewable energy, smart cities.

4

Systems, Design & Innovation

Systems engineering, design optimization, Industry 4.0, supply chain, human-centered engineering.

5

Applied & Industrial Engineering

Process optimization, healthcare engineering applications, resilient infrastructure, tech transfer.

Author Guidelines & Editorial Workflow

Every manuscript submitted to JEC follows a structured, transparent path from submission to publication. Prepare your manuscript per standard scholarly formatting (structured abstract, keywords, IMRaD body, references, declarations), then submit online for editorial processing.

1

Manuscript Submission

Author submits the manuscript online with cover letter and declarations.

2

Editorial Screening

Editor-in-Chief checks scope fit, formatting, and plagiarism screening.

3

Double-Blind Peer Review

Independent reviewers assess originality, rigor, and significance.

4

Editorial Decision

Accept, minor/major revision, or reject — communicated to the author.

5

Revision & Resubmission

Authors respond to reviewer comments and resubmit the revised manuscript.

6

Publication & Indexing

Accepted articles are copyedited, published online, and submitted for indexing.
