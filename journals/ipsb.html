Scope of the Journal

IPSB promotes innovative scientific and technological developments that address complex challenges in healthcare, materials science, energy systems, and environmental sustainability, encouraging cross-disciplinary integration between physical sciences, biological systems, and engineering.

1

Physical Sciences

Applied & theoretical physics, condensed matter physics, optics/photonics, quantum science, thermodynamics.

2

Chemical & Materials Sciences

Physical chemistry, nanomaterials, surface chemistry & catalysis, polymer science, energy materials.

3

Bioengineering & Life Science Interfaces

Biophysics, biomaterials & tissue engineering, bio-nano interfaces, medical devices, mechanobiology.

4

Computational & Engineering Sciences

Computational modeling, AI/ML in physical & biomedical systems, multiscale modeling, instrumentation.

5

Medical & Applied Technologies

Medical physics, diagnostic imaging, biosensors, drug delivery systems, wearable healthcare tech.

6

Energy, Environment & Emerging Tech

Renewable energy systems, environmental remediation, green chemistry, advanced manufacturing.

Article Types Welcomed

Original Research Articles Review Articles Short Communications Systematic Reviews & Meta-Analyses Editorials & Perspectives Letters to the Editor Technical Notes & Engineering Reports Data Papers Methods & Protocol Papers

Author Guidelines & Editorial Workflow

Every manuscript submitted to IPSB follows a structured, transparent path from submission to publication. Prepare your manuscript per standard scholarly formatting (structured abstract, keywords, IMRaD body, references, declarations), then submit online for editorial processing.

1

Manuscript Submission

Author submits the manuscript online with cover letter and declarations.

2

Editorial Screening

Editorial office checks scope fit, formatting, and plagiarism screening.

3

Double-Blind Peer Review

Independent reviewers assess originality, rigor, and significance.

4

Editorial Decision

Accept, minor/major revision, or reject — communicated to the author.

5

Revision & Resubmission

Authors respond to reviewer comments and resubmit the revised manuscript.

6

Publication & Indexing

Accepted articles are copyedited, published online, and submitted for indexing.
