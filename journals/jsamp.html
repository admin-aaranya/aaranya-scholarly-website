Scope of the Journal

JSAMP covers strategic, managerial, organizational, and advisory domains — including finance, operations, marketing, entrepreneurship, and sectoral practice — welcoming contributions from academicians, consultants, executives, and policy professionals alike.

1

Corporate strategy, strategic planning, business transformation, M&A, business model innovation.

Author Guidelines & Editorial Workflow

Every manuscript submitted to JSAMP follows a structured, transparent path from submission to publication. Prepare your manuscript per standard scholarly formatting (structured abstract, keywords, body, references, declarations), then submit online for editorial processing.

1

Manuscript Submission

Author submits the manuscript online with cover letter and declarations.

2

Editorial Screening

Editorial office checks scope fit, formatting, and plagiarism screening.

3

Double-Blind Peer Review

Independent reviewers assess originality, rigor, and significance.

4

Editorial Decision

Accept, minor/major revision, or reject — communicated to the author.

5

Revision & Resubmission

Authors respond to reviewer comments and resubmit the revised manuscript.

6

Publication & Indexing

Accepted articles are copyedited, published online, and submitted for indexing.
