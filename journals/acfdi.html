Scope of the Journal

ACFDI integrates computer science theory, computational engineering, data analytics, AI, cybersecurity, human-centered systems, and digital innovation frameworks to solve complex, evolving real-world problems across scientific, industrial, enterprise, and societal contexts.

1

Computational Science & Core Systems

Theoretical computer science, algorithms, distributed/parallel computing, high-performance computing.

2

Artificial Intelligence & Intelligent Systems

Machine learning, deep learning, explainable AI, knowledge representation, autonomous systems.

3

Data Science & Computational Analytics

Predictive analytics, big data systems, data mining, statistical learning, visual analytics.

4

Software Engineering & Digital Platforms

Software architecture, DevOps, cloud-native applications, enterprise systems, software quality.

5

Cybersecurity & Digital Trust

Information assurance, network security, cryptography, digital privacy, threat intelligence.

6

Robotics, Automation & Cyber-Physical Systems

Intelligent robotics, industrial automation, embedded systems, IoT, digital twins.

Author Guidelines & Editorial Workflow

Every manuscript submitted to ACFDI follows a structured, transparent path from submission to publication. Prepare your manuscript per standard scholarly formatting (structured abstract, keywords, body, references, declarations; code/data availability statements encouraged), then submit online for editorial processing.

1

Manuscript Submission

Author submits the manuscript online with cover letter and declarations.

2

Editorial Screening

Editorial office checks scope fit, formatting, and plagiarism screening.

3

Double-Blind Peer Review

Independent reviewers assess originality, rigor, and significance.

4

Editorial Decision

Accept, minor/major revision, or reject — communicated to the author.

5

Revision & Resubmission

Authors respond to reviewer comments and resubmit the revised manuscript.

6

Publication & Indexing

Accepted articles are copyedited, published online, and submitted for indexing.
