Scope of the Journal

GHESB takes a holistic, integrative approach — connecting laboratory science, field studies, and public health interventions — aligning with global priorities such as climate change, emerging diseases, and sustainable resource management.

1

Global Health & Public Health Sciences

Epidemiology, infectious & non-communicable disease, health systems & policy, health equity.

2

Environmental & Ecological Sciences

Environmental biology, climate change & health, biodiversity, toxicology, ecosystem sustainability.

3

Sustainable Biosciences

Sustainable agriculture, plant sciences, agricultural biotechnology, bioeconomy, natural resources.

4

Medical & Biological Interfaces

Immunology, nutrition & metabolism, environmental microbiology, One Health, zoonotic disease.

5

Traditional & Integrative Medicine

Herbal & plant-based therapeutics, ethnopharmacology, evidence-based traditional medicine.

6

Technology, Policy & Emerging Areas

Digital & public health informatics, AI in health/environment, SDG policy research, disaster resilience.

Article Types Welcomed

Original Research Articles Review Articles Short Communications Case Studies Systematic Reviews & Meta-Analyses Editorials & Perspectives Letters to the Editor Technical Notes & Field Reports Data Papers Methods & Protocol Papers

Author Guidelines & Editorial Workflow

Every manuscript submitted to GHESB follows a structured, transparent path from submission to publication. Prepare your manuscript per standard scholarly formatting (structured abstract, keywords, IMRaD body, references, declarations), then submit online for editorial processing.

1

Manuscript Submission

Author submits the manuscript online with cover letter and declarations.

2

Editorial Screening

Editor-in-Chief checks scope fit, formatting, and plagiarism screening.

3

Double-Blind Peer Review

Independent reviewers assess originality, rigor, and significance.

4

Editorial Decision

Accept, minor/major revision, or reject — communicated to the author.

5

Revision & Resubmission

Authors respond to reviewer comments and resubmit the revised manuscript.

6

Publication & Indexing

Accepted articles are copyedited, published online, and submitted for indexing.
